## How to play

1. Place chips on the table layout: on a single number, a group of numbers, red or black, odd or even.
2. The dealer spins the wheel and sends the ball the other way round the rim.
3. When the dealer calls no more bets, the layout is closed until the ball settles.
4. The dealer marks the winning number, pays the winning bets and clears the rest.

Bets on a single number pay the most and win the least often. Bets on red or black win more often and pay less.
