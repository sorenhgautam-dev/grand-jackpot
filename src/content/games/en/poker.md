## How to play

1. Each player receives their own cards face down.
2. Five shared cards are dealt face up in three stages: the flop, the turn and the river. The table is marked for each.
3. There is a round of betting before and after each stage. You can bet, call, raise or fold.
4. The best five-card hand from your own cards and the shared cards wins, or the last player still in the hand.

Hands rank from high card at the bottom to a royal flush at the top. Ask the dealer for this table’s rules and payouts.
