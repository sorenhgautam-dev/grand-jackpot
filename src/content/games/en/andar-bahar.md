## How to play

1. The dealer turns one card face up in the middle of the table. This is the joker.
2. Bet on Andar (inside) or Bahar (outside).
3. The dealer deals cards one at a time, to Andar and Bahar in turn, until a card of the same value as the joker appears.
4. The side that receives the matching card wins.

The layout also has second-bet boxes. The dealer can explain them before the deal.
