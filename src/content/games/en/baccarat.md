## How to play

1. Before the deal, place your bet on Player, Banker or Tie.
2. The dealer deals two cards to each hand. A third card may follow by fixed rules, so you make no choices once the cards are out.
3. Cards from 2 to 9 count at face value, tens and picture cards count as zero, and aces count as one. Only the last digit of a total counts, so a 7 and an 8 make 5.
4. The hand closer to 9 wins. If the two hands are equal, the result is a tie.

The table layout also takes Super Six bets. Ask the dealer how they pay before you place one.
