## How to play

1. Each player is dealt a hand of cards.
2. On your turn, draw a card and discard one, working your hand into sequences (consecutive cards of one suit) and sets (cards of the same value).
3. When every card in your hand belongs to a valid sequence or set, declare.
4. The first player to make a valid declaration wins the hand.

Marriage is played between players. Ask the dealer about the house rules for this table.
