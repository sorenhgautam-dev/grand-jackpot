## How to play

1. Place your bet. You and the dealer each receive two cards; one of the dealer’s cards stays face down.
2. Cards from 2 to 10 count at face value, picture cards count as 10, and an ace counts as 1 or 11.
3. Ask for another card (hit) or keep your hand (stand). Go over 21 and the hand is lost.
4. The dealer then plays the hand by a fixed rule and the closer hand to 21 wins.

Ask the dealer for this table’s rules on splitting pairs and doubling down.
