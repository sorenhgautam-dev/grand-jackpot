This is a preview of the Grand Jackpot Casino website.

## What the site collects

Nothing. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies’ servers.

## Forms

The contact and package forms check what you type in your browser. They don’t send it anywhere, and nothing is stored. With JavaScript turned off, sending a form opens a confirmation page, and the form’s contents still stay in your browser.

## Your browser

The home page remembers, until you close the tab, that you’ve already seen its opening animation. This is kept in your browser’s session storage and never leaves your device.

## Hosting

The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

## Links to other sites

The Directions link opens Google Maps or your maps app, which has its own privacy policy.
