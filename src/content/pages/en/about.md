## The building

Grand Jackpot Casino stands in the grounds of Grand Hotel Kathmandu at Soaltee Mode. The front of the building carries the four card suits in large panels, and the entrance sits under a copper-slatted arch with the Grand Jackpot sign.

Inside, the live tables, the slots, the buffet and the seating share one floor under curved bands of light. Dealers run the tables in the Normal Pit and the VIP Pit.

## Grand Hotel Kathmandu

The hotel has rooms, dining, meeting and event spaces, including the Sapphire Ballroom, and a swimming pool. The casino can arrange a room as part of the VIP package.
