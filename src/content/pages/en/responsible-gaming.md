## Entry and ID

Entry is for foreign passport holders only. Bring your passport; reception checks it before you go onto the floor.

## Keep it to entertainment

Casino games are games of chance, and every game gives the house an edge over time. The longer you play, the more likely you are to lose. Treat what you spend as the price of a night out, not as a way to make money.

- Set a budget before you arrive, and stop when it’s spent.
- Decide when you’ll leave.
- Don’t try to win back what you’ve lost.
- Don’t play with borrowed money, or money meant for anything else.
- Take breaks. Eat, step outside, check the time.
- Alcohol makes limits harder to keep.

## Signs to watch for

- Spending more, or playing for longer, than you meant to.
- Hiding how much you play.
- Feeling you have to win money back.
- Playing to get away from other problems.

## Getting help

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone close to you, talk to someone you trust and contact a support service in your home country. You can speak to our staff at reception at any time.
