# Design sheet

**Voice:** assured, warm, unhurried — an international house with a Nepali host.
**Signature element:** the tiered *pagoda roofline* (logo mark, section dividers) and *jharokha-style arches* framing imagery and icons, over a faint Dhaka-inspired diamond lattice.

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | `#090908` | base background |
| emerald-deep / emerald | `#05211B` / `#094031` | alternating sections, glows |
| gold | `#C9A24B` (light `#ECD392`, dark `#8C6826`) | accents, rules, buttons |
| ivory | `#F4EDDD` | primary text |
| muted | `#B2AB99` | secondary text (AA on ink) |

**Type:** Bodoni Moda (display, high-contrast and Art Deco-leaning) + Manrope (UI and body).
**Motion:** one orchestrated hero entrance and a slow roulette backdrop; scroll reveals; hover/focus feedback at 200–300 ms. Honours `prefers-reduced-motion` (fades only).
**Imagery:** abstract illustrated placeholders, each tagged as a placeholder, to be replaced by real photography.
